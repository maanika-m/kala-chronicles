# KALĀ CHRONICLES

### Indian Art Through the Ages

KALĀ CHRONICLES is an interactive digital museum experience exploring the evolution, geographic diversity, and creative fusion of Indian art.

The project brings three academic activities together into one unified website:

- **Interactive Timeline with Artifacts**
- **Interactive Art Map**
- **Regional Painting Fusion**

Rather than presenting the activities as separate projects, KALĀ CHRONICLES connects **time, place, and artistic creation** into a single experience.

---

## ✦ Features

### 01 — Interactive Timeline

Explore Indian art chronologically across six major periods:

- Indus Valley Civilization
- Mauryan / Early Buddhist Art
- Gupta Period
- Medieval / Temple Art
- Mughal / Rajput / Regional Painting
- Modern & Contemporary Art

Users can explore representative artifacts and artworks, open detailed information, and navigate through the historical timeline.

Each artifact includes information such as:

- Name
- Date / Period
- Material
- Location
- Description
- Historical Context
- Artistic Significance

---

### 02 — Interactive Art Map

Explore the geographical spread of Indian art through an interactive map of India.

The map highlights regions associated with important artistic traditions, movements, and artworks.

Users can explore locations and discover:

- Regional art traditions
- Important artists
- Representative artworks
- Historical background
- Cultural significance

The map connects geographical context with the artworks explored throughout the timeline.

---

### 03 — Regional Painting Fusion

The project also explores the creative combination of different Indian regional painting traditions.

The fusion project combines:

**Warli × Phad**

The section introduces the characteristics of both traditions and presents their creative synthesis through a new composition.

The fusion demonstrates how elements such as:

- Figurative forms
- Composition
- Patterns
- Colour
- Narrative structure
- Decorative motifs

can be combined while retaining recognizable characteristics of their respective traditions.

---

## ✦ Design Philosophy

KALĀ CHRONICLES is designed as a **digital museum rather than a conventional educational website**.

The visual language takes inspiration from archival collections, museum exhibitions, and historical documentation.

The interface emphasizes:

- Visual storytelling
- Chronological exploration
- Cultural context
- Artifact-focused presentation
- Minimal and immersive navigation

---

## ✦ Technology

Built using:

- **HTML**
- **CSS**
- **JavaScript**
- **Responsive Web Design**
- **AI-assisted design and development**

The interface was initially designed using **Google Stitch** and subsequently developed into an interactive web experience.
